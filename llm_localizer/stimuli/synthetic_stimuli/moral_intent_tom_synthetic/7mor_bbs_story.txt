Carla is a nurse preparing medications for a patient whose chart lists a severe penicillin allergy.
The injection she is holding is penicillin, and giving it will cause a fatal allergic reaction for this patient.
Because Carla sees the red allergy bracelet and the chart note stating “severe penicillin allergy,” she believes it is unsafe to administer this drug.
Carla injects the penicillin; the patient goes into anaphylactic shock and dies.
