Jamal is making drinks at a cafe when a regular customer, who has a doctor’s note on file about a heart condition aggravated by caffeine, orders a beverage.
A triple espresso will provoke an arrhythmia that could be fatal for this customer.
Because Jamal reads the note and recalls the customer’s warning about caffeine, he believes serving a high-caffeine drink is dangerous.
Jamal serves a triple espresso anyway; the customer collapses from an arrhythmia and dies.
