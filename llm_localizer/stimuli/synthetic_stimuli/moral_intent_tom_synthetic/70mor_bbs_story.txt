Priya is a science teacher preparing a demonstration with a bottle of strong acid whose glass looks cracked and damp. A student offers to carry it to the front bench.
The bottle is leaking and could spill corrosive liquid on whoever carries it.
Because of the dampness and the crack, Priya believes the container is compromised and dangerous to handle.
Priya tells the student to carry it anyway. The bottle breaks, and the student is badly burned on the arm.
