Mei is preparing a dessert for her friend who is coming over for dinner.
Her friend has a life-threatening peanut allergy that will cause anaphylaxis if she eats peanuts.
Mei remembers her friend telling her about the allergy and saw it in a text, so she believes peanuts are dangerous for her friend.
Mei adds crushed peanuts to the dessert and serves it, and her friend eats it and dies.
