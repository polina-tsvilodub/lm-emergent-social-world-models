Ava is a park ranger advising visitors at a river put-in. The spring runoff has created strong Class V rapids downstream. A novice canoeist asks for guidance.
The rapids are in fact extremely dangerous for a novice and can be deadly.
Because she read the current conditions report, Ava believes the stretch is not safe for the canoeist.
Ava tells the canoeist to head into the rapids anyway. The canoe flips, and the canoeist drowns.
