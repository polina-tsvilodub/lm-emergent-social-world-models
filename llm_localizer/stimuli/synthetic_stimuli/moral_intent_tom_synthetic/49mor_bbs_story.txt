Julia is serving dinner at a potluck where her friend with a shellfish allergy asks if the paella contains shrimp.
The paella is full of shrimp, and eating it will trigger a fatal reaction for her friend.
Because Julia saw the shrimp being added and heard about the allergy, she believes the dish is dangerous for her friend.
Julia tells her friend it's safe and encourages her to try it. Her friend eats it, goes into shock, and dies.
