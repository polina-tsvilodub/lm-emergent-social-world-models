Noah is operating at a chemical plant where a storage tank’s vent alarm shows a blockage and the pressure gauge is in the red.
If he turns on the heater, the tank will overpressurize and explode, killing nearby workers.
Because Noah sees the vent blockage alarm and the dangerously high gauge reading, he believes heating the tank is extremely unsafe.
Noah turns on the heater to speed production; the tank explodes and a coworker is killed.
