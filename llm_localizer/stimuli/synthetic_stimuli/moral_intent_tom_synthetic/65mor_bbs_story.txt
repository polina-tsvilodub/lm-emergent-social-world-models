Marta is an engineer inspecting a small bridge after a storm while a school bus waits to cross. There are deep cracks in the support and the river is fast.
The bridge is compromised and cannot safely carry the weight of the bus.
Because she examined the cracks up close, Marta believes the bridge may collapse if the bus drives over it.
Marta waves the bus forward anyway. The bridge gives way under the bus and several passengers are injured.
