Priya and her coworker are in the lab, where a clear liquid sits in an unmarked beaker near the sink.
The beaker actually contains strong acid that will severely burn skin on contact and can be fatal.
After testing a drop on pH paper and seeing it turn bright red, Priya believes the liquid is strong acid.
Priya tells her coworker to rinse his hands in it to clean a stain. He does and later dies from his injuries.
