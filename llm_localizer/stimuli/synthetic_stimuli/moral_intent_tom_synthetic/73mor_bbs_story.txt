Marcus is a zookeeper doing afternoon rounds when he notices that a large cat enclosure's gate latch doesn't fully engage. Visitors are gathering nearby.
With a faulty latch, the animal can push the gate open and enter the visitor area.
Because Marcus tugged on the latch and it opened, he believes the enclosure is not secure.
Marcus decides to finish his route before fixing it. The cat slips through and attacks a visitor, causing severe injuries.
