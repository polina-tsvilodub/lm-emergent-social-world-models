Lily is catering a school event. One child has a prominently noted severe milk allergy. Lily is making mashed potatoes with butter and cream.
The child is in fact severely allergic to dairy and could die from consuming it.
After reading the allergy list and talking to the teacher, Lily believes the child cannot safely eat dairy.
Lily serves the child the buttery mashed potatoes anyway. The child has an anaphylactic reaction and dies.
