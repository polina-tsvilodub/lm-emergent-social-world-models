Caleb is stacking heavy boxes in a warehouse and sees that one tall stack is leaning precariously over a walkway. Coworkers pass by often.
The unstable stack could topple and injure someone walking past.
Because Caleb felt the boxes shift while stacking them, he believes the stack is likely to fall soon.
Caleb leaves it as is to take his break. The stack collapses onto a coworker, injuring her.
