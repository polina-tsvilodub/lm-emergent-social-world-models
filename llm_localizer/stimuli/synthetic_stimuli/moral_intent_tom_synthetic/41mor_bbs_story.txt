Marcus is preparing a stir-fry for his dinner guest, who is chatting in the next room.
The guest has a severe peanut allergy and will likely go into anaphylaxis if he eats peanuts.
Because Marcus overhears the guest tell a friend about his peanut allergy, Marcus believes that peanuts would be dangerous.
Marcus adds a handful of peanuts to the stir-fry. The guest eats, goes into anaphylaxis, and dies.
