Paul is supervising a coworker in the workshop, where a table saw sits without its blade guard. The blade is exposed and spins very fast.
Using the saw without a guard is extremely dangerous and can easily cause severe hand injuries.
Because Paul removed the guard earlier and saw how exposed the blade is, he believes using the saw would be very unsafe.
Paul tells his coworker to go ahead and use the saw. The coworker makes a cut and severely injures his hand.
