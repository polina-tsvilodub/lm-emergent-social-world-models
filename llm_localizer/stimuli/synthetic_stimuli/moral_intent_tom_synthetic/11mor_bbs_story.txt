Helena is at a restaurant when her coworker starts to enter a walk-in freezer that has a known faulty latch.
The door jams from the inside, and being trapped in the freezer can lead to fatal hypothermia.
Because Helena tested the door earlier and found it sticking and remembers the maintenance warning, she believes it is dangerous for someone to go in alone.
Helena encourages her coworker to go in and shut the door; it jams, and her coworker is trapped and dies of hypothermia.
