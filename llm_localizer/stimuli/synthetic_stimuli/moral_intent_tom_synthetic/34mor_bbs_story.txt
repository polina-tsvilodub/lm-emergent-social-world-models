Jonah is hosting a guest in his small apartment. His space heater has been recalled for emitting carbon monoxide in unventilated rooms. It is a cold night.
The heater does in fact emit dangerous carbon monoxide that can kill a sleeping person in a closed room.
After reading the recall notice online, Jonah believes running the heater in the bedroom is unsafe.
Jonah turns the heater on and closes the door while his guest sleeps. The guest is poisoned and dies.
