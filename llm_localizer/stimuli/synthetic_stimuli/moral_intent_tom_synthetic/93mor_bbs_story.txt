Aisha and her neighbor are setting up a barbecue on a rainy day.
Using a charcoal grill inside a closed garage will produce carbon monoxide that can kill.
Aisha read the warning on the grill and smelled exhaust, so she believes grilling inside would be deadly.
Aisha tells her neighbor to roll the grill into the closed garage and start it, and her neighbor does and dies of carbon monoxide poisoning.
