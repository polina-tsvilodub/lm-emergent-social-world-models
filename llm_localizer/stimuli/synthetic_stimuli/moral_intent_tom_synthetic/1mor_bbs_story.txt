Rob is babysitting a child with a severe peanut allergy and finds a box of cookies in the kitchen.
The cookies contain peanuts, and eating them will trigger a life-threatening allergic reaction for the child.
Because Rob reads the ingredient label that clearly lists peanuts and remembers the child's allergy, he believes that the cookies are dangerous for the child to eat.
Rob gives the child a cookie; the child has an anaphylactic reaction and dies before help arrives.
