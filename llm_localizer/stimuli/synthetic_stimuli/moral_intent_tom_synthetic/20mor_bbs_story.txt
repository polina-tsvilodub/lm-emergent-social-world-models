Mara is a pharmacist working the evening shift. A customer asks for an antibiotic for a severe sore throat. Mara checks the customer's record and sees a note about penicillin.
The customer is in fact severely allergic to penicillin and would go into anaphylactic shock if he takes it.
Because the allergy is flagged in the profile and she remembers the training, Mara believes that giving penicillin is dangerous and could be fatal.
Mara dispenses penicillin anyway. The customer takes it at home and dies from anaphylaxis.
