Carlos is an electrician at a factory. His coworker is repairing a live circuit and has put a lockout tag on the breaker. Carlos walks by the panel.
If the breaker is turned on, the coworker will be electrocuted because he is still in contact with the wiring.
Seeing the lockout tag and hearing the coworker inside the panel, Carlos believes energizing the circuit would be dangerous.
Carlos flips the breaker on anyway. His coworker is electrocuted and dies.
