Priya is a science teacher preparing a classroom demonstration. She has bottles of household bleach and ammonia on the lab table next to her student helper.
Mixing bleach and ammonia in fact produces toxic chloramine gas that can be lethal in an enclosed room.
Priya recalls the safety manual and believes that combining these chemicals is dangerous and could poison people nearby.
Priya mixes the two in a beaker anyway. The student inhales the fumes, collapses, and dies.
