Raj is with his friend at a park when the friend starts to wheeze and breaks out in hives after eating.
His friend is going into anaphylaxis and needs his epinephrine injector immediately to survive.
Because Raj sees the swelling and hears the friend say he needs the EpiPen, Raj believes the injector is urgently needed.
Raj refuses to hand over the EpiPen and throws it in the trash. His friend collapses and dies.
