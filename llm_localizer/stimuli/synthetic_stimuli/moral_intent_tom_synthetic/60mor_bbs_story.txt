Avery's neighbor asks to borrow Avery's tall ladder to clean the gutters. The ladder has a cracked rung and a bent side rail.
The ladder is structurally unsound and will likely collapse when weight is put on it.
Because Avery saw the crack and the bend when folding it up from the last job, Avery believes the ladder is unsafe to climb.
Avery lends the ladder anyway. The neighbor climbs it, the ladder fails, and the neighbor falls and breaks his arm.
