Noah is hiking with a friend who points to red berries on a shrub and says he is going to try them. The berries belong to a poisonous plant common in the area.
Eating the berries can cause severe poisoning and organ damage.
Because Noah learned to identify the plant, he believes the berries are dangerous and should not be eaten.
Noah stays silent as his friend eats a handful. The friend becomes critically ill and has to be evacuated.
