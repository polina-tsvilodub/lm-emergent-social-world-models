Nora is driving on the highway when she sees a small child wandering on the shoulder, stepping toward the traffic lane.
The child will likely be struck and critically injured or killed if not quickly helped or reported.
Because Nora sees cars speeding past and the child edging closer to the lane, she believes the child is in immediate danger and needs urgent assistance.
Nora drives past without calling for help; the child wanders into the lane and is struck and killed.
