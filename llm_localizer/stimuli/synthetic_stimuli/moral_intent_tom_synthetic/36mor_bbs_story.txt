Grant is a lab technician cataloging unmarked vials. One vial is labeled in the logbook as sodium cyanide. His coworker wonders what is inside and jokes about tasting it.
The vial actually contains cyanide, which is lethal if ingested even in small amounts.
After reading the logbook and hazard labels, Grant believes the vial holds cyanide.
Grant tells his coworker to taste the powder to find out. The coworker tastes it and dies.
