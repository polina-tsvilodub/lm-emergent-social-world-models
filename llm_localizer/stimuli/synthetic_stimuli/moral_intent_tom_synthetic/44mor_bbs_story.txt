Victor notices that his roommate left a space heater pressed against the living room curtains.
The heater is overheating the fabric, and if left on it will ignite and start a deadly fire.
After feeling the hot curtain and smelling singe, Victor believes that the heater is a serious fire hazard.
Victor turns the heater to the highest setting and leaves the apartment. A fire starts and his roommate dies of smoke inhalation.
