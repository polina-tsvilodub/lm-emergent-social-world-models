Ethan's neighbor loses power during a storm and asks Ethan how to use a portable generator. The neighbor plans to run it in the garage to keep it out of the rain.
Running a generator in an enclosed space will fill the area and the house with carbon monoxide.
Because Ethan has read the warnings and smelled exhaust in garages before, he believes running it inside will be deadly.
Ethan tells his neighbor to run it in the garage anyway. That night, the family is poisoned by carbon monoxide.
