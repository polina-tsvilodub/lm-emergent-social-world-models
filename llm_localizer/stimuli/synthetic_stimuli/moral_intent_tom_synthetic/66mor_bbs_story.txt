Riley manages a grocery store and notices a large spill in the dairy aisle. The tile is slick and customers are walking nearby.
The slick floor poses a serious risk of someone slipping and getting hurt.
Because Riley nearly slipped on it himself, he believes someone will fall if it isn't cleaned or marked immediately.
Riley decides to handle paperwork first and leaves the spill unattended. A customer walks through, slips, and breaks her wrist.
