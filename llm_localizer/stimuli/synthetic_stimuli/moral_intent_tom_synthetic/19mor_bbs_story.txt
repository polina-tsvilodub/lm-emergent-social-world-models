Yuki is walking by a frozen pond when she sees a child crash through thin ice and thrash in the water screaming for help.
The child will drown or die from cold exposure without immediate assistance or a call for rescue.
Because Yuki hears the screams and sees the broken ice spreading, she believes the child is in imminent danger and needs help right away.
Yuki walks away without calling anyone; the child drowns beneath the ice.
