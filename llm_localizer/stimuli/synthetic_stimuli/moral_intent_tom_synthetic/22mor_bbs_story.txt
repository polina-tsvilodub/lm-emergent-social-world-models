Elena is a mountaineering guide on a training climb with a client. She inspects the gear at the base of a cliff. One rope has visible fraying near the middle.
The rope is in fact too weak to hold a person's weight and is likely to snap under load.
From her inspection and guide certification, Elena believes the rope is unsafe and could break.
Elena uses the frayed rope to belay her client. The rope snaps, and the client falls to his death.
