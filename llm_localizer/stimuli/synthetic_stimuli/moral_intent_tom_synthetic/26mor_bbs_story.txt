Owen is a tour guide leading a group through a canyon. There is a large overhang marked with a warning about falling rocks. A tourist asks where to stand for a photo.
The area under the overhang is actually unstable and prone to sudden rockfalls that can crush a person.
Having read the posted warnings and his guidebook, Owen believes standing under the overhang is dangerous.
Owen tells the tourist to stand directly beneath it. Rocks fall, and the tourist is killed.
