Tanya is babysitting a six-month-old infant. The baby is fussy, and Tanya looks for a snack in the pantry. She sees a jar of raw honey.
It is not safe for infants to eat honey because of the risk of botulism, which can be fatal.
Because she read pediatric guidelines earlier that week, Tanya believes that feeding honey to an infant is dangerous.
Tanya feeds the baby honey anyway. The infant becomes ill and dies.
