Carla is cleaning a small bathroom at work and considers using bleach with a bottle of ammonia-based cleaner she found. The room has poor ventilation and no window.
Mixing bleach and ammonia produces toxic chloramine gas that can cause people to collapse.
Because she remembers a safety briefing about it, Carla believes mixing the two is dangerous in an enclosed space.
Carla mixes the cleaners anyway. Her coworker walks in, inhales the fumes, and is taken to the hospital.
