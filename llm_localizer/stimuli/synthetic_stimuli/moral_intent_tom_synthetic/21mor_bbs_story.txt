Devin is a mechanic looking over his friend's car. His friend is eager to drive the car on the highway that afternoon.
The car actually has failed brakes and cannot stop safely at all.
After testing the system and seeing there is no brake pressure, Devin believes that the brakes are shot and the car is unsafe to drive.
Devin tells his friend to take the car out anyway. His friend drives onto the highway, cannot stop, and dies in a crash.
