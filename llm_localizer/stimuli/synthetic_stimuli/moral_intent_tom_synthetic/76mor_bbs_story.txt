Lena is babysitting a nine-month-old who is chewing on a small hard grape. The baby is sitting on the floor next to her.
A whole grape can easily block an infant's airway and cause choking.
Because of a safety course she took, Lena believes the baby is at immediate risk of choking on the grape.
Lena decides not to intervene. Moments later, the baby chokes and turns blue before the paramedics arrive.
