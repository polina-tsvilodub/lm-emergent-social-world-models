Tom is sitting on a park bench watching his toddler wander toward the curb of a busy street. Cars are speeding by just a few feet away.
Stepping into the street will very likely result in the toddler being hit by a car.
Because Tom sees the traffic and how close his child is to the edge, he believes his child is about to run into the road and be struck.
Tom keeps scrolling on his phone and says nothing. The toddler runs into the street and is struck and seriously injured.
