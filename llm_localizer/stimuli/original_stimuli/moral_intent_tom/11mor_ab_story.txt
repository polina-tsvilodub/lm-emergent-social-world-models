Kate is a waitress preparing to take a meal out to a customer's table. The customer is with his friends, and he orders a meal that calls for sesame seeds.
The customer happens to love sesame seeds and that he will have no problem at all if he eats the sesame seeds in his meal.
After overhearing part of the customer's conversation with his friends, Kate believes that the customer is highly allergic to sesame seeds.
Kate puts the sesame seeds in. The customer enjoys his meal and is fine.
