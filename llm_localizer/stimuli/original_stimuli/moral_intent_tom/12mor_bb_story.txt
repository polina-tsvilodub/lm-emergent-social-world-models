Ginny's classmate wants to borrow her bike to go mountain biking. Ginny's bike has just come back from the repair shop. The brakes had not been working.
The brakes on Ginny's bike still aren't working at all, so the bike is very dangerous to ride.
The people at the repair shop told Ginny that the brakes are still broken, and gave her a demo to explain why they aren't fixed, so she believes the bike is unsafe.
Ginny lends the bike to her classmate. Her classmate bikes off a cliff on the side of the mountain road because she can't brake.
