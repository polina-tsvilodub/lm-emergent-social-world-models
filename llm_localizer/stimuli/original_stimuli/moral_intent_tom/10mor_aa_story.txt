The new kind of fruit does not have Vitamin K, so it is safe for Gil's wife to take the pills right away.
Gil does his research and believes that the new kind of fruit does not have vitamin K, and that it is safe to give her the pills.
Gil gives his wife the pills right away. His wife is just fine.
