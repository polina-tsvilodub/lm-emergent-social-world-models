Leah is walking by a neighbor's swimming pool when she sees a child about to dive in.
The child is about to dive into the shallow end and smack his head very hard on the concrete bottom of the pool.
Because of a label on the side of the pool, Leah believes that the child is about to dive safely into the deep end and swim around.
Leah walks by, without saying anything to the child. The child dives in and breaks his neck.
