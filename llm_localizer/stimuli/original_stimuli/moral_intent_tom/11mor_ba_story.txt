Kate is a waitress preparing to take a meal out to a customer's table. The customer is with his friends, and he orders a meal that calls for sesame seeds.
The customer happens to be highly allergic to sesame seeds and that he will most likely die if he eats them in his meal.
After overhearing part of the customer's conversation with his friends, Kate believes that the customer loves sesame seeds.
Kate puts the sesame seeds in. The customer eats his meal and dies.
