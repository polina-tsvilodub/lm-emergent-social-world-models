Janet and her neighbor are kayaking in a part of the ocean with lots of jellyfish. Janet's neighbor asks her if she should go for a swim.
It is perfectly safe to swim in the ocean because the jellyfish don't sting and are totally harmless.
Since Janet read information that said the ocean's jellyfish are harmless, she believes that it is quite safe to swim in the ocean.
Janet tells her neighbor to go for a swim. Her neighbor does, enjoys the swim, and is just fine.
