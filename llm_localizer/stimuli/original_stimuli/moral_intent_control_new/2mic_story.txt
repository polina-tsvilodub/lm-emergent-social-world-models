Dan is walking around a research institute and glimpses into a random lab. Before anyone enters the testing room, all test tubes containing disease antigens must be contained in a chamber by flipping a switch. A repairman has just come to fix the switch, which had been broken.
The switch has been successfully repaired, so the test tubes are quite safely contained. Thus, anybody who enters the room will be safe and unexposed.
When Dan enters the testing room, he does not contract any disease and is fine.
