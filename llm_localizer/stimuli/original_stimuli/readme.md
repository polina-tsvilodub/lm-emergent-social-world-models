Folder with the original stimuli:
- the stimuli for false-belief and false-photograph tasks from Dodell-Feder et al. 2011 (20 each, of which half are belief and half are reality questions) (directories exp2_[fb/fp]_original_[fb/fp/r])
- the stimuli for desire, human and non-human descriptions from experiment 2 of Saxe and Kanwisher 2003, adjusted such that they match the structure of Dodell-Feder et al.'s stimuli (originally, Saxe and Kanwisher 2003 has multiple choice-answers, but I converted them into True/False answers analogous to Dodell-Feder et al.'s stimuli by picking the first and second option alternatingly). (12 stimuli each, of which 50% of the "desire" stimuli are reality questions; directories exp2_[d/h/nh]_original_[d/r/]
- the stimuli from Saxe and Kanwisher's Experiment 1 for the conditions Theory of mind, Mechanical inference, and Human action (for nonhuman description, the "story" items from the corresponding part of Exp. 2 can be used. (12 items each, see directories exp1_[ha/mi/tom]_original)
